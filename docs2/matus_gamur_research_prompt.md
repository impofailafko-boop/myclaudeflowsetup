4. **FLARE (Forward-Looking Active Retrieval)** - Identify when more research is needed
5. **Chain of Verification (CoVe)** - Verify each major finding
6. **Self-Consistency (SC)** - Cross-check facts from multiple angles
7. **System 2 Attention (S2A)** - Filter noise, focus on signal
